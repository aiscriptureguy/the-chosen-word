## Suggested Study Method

For each row:

1. Read the New Testament passage in context.
2. Read the Old Testament source in context.
3. Note whether Christ quotes the passage exactly, combines passages, or applies
   it to a new setting.
4. Record what the quotation teaches about Christ, covenant, worship,
   discipleship, or judgment.
