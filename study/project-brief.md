# The Chosen Word — Project Brief

**Status:** Gate 1 approved 2026-10-22 — Phase 2 beginning
**Last updated:** 2026-10-20

---

## Study Topic

Every instance in which Jesus Christ directly quotes or closely alludes to an Old Testament scripture — across the four Gospels and Third Nephi. The central conviction driving the study: Christ is the most perfect being to ever live, fully aligned with the Father, and the scriptures he chose to cite were not chosen casually. They represent a kind of canon-within-the-canon — passages of eternal significance and spiritual importance selected by the one being who understood all scripture perfectly.

---

## Scope

- **Texts included:** Matthew, Mark, Luke, John, and 3 Nephi (Book of Mormon)
- **Speaker filter:** Only instances where Christ himself is the speaker — not narrator commentary, not quotations by disciples or opponents (unless Christ affirms or corrects them)
- **Quotation types included:** Direct quotations and close allusions (explicit citations with "it is written" or equivalent formulas, paraphrases that invoke a specific OT text, and verbal allusions close enough that the source text is clearly identifiable)
- **Quotation types excluded:** Loose thematic echoes where no specific OT passage is being invoked; OT language absorbed into general idiom without clear citation intent
- **D&C:** Deferred — will evaluate after Phase 1 produces a count. If the number is manageable, D&C instances where Christ speaks in first person will be added in a second research pass

---

## Non-Goals

- Not a comprehensive biblical commentary on the OT passages themselves
- Not covering every OT allusion made by Gospel narrators *about* Christ (e.g., fulfillment formulas like "that it might be fulfilled")
- Not reproducing ESV text on the site — ESV used for reference and comparison only, accessed via outbound links
- Not a devotional reading plan or Come Follow Me curriculum product
- Not attempting to rank scriptural importance beyond what the research supports

---

## Audience

Primary: Chase's own deep scripture study — a reference tool he returns to repeatedly.
Secondary: Other members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints who may be shown the finished site.

---

## Key Questions

These drive the study and should each be answered with traceable evidence:

1. **Catalog** — What is the complete set of direct quotations and close allusions Christ makes to OT scripture across the Gospels and 3 Nephi?
2. **Distribution** — Which OT books does Christ cite most frequently? Is there a pattern in which books he draws from?
3. **Repetition** — Does Christ quote the same passage more than once, and if so, across what different contexts or audiences?
4. **Purpose** — Why did Christ consider each scripture worth quoting? What does each choice reveal about his mission, his priorities, or the spiritual importance of the underlying text?
5. **Context sensitivity** — How does the audience or setting (private disciples, hostile questioners, synagogue teaching, Sermon on the Mount, Last Supper, trial) affect which scriptures he cites?
6. **Absence** — Are there OT books, themes, or entire sections conspicuously absent from Christ's quotations? What might that suggest?
7. **3 Nephi as control** — What does the 3 Nephi record add or confirm? Because Christ *dictated* what should be written there, does it show a different selection pattern than the Gospel accounts?

---

## Preliminary Content Types

Each entry in the site will need:

- **2-word key** — a scannable label for the scripture (e.g., "Bread Alone," "Corner Stone," "My God")
- **Short summary** — one or two sentences: what Christ said, where it came from, and why it mattered in that moment
- **Full passage view** — the Church scripture text of both the NT citation and the OT source; outbound ESV link for comparison
- **Study questions answered** — per the four questions above, applied to each individual passage
- **Metadata for filtering:** OT book, NT/BoM location, quotation type, audience/context, whether repeated

## Unresolved Decisions

1. **Allusion boundary** — We need a working definition of "close allusion" vs. "thematic echo" to apply consistently during Phase 1 cataloging. Proposed: a close allusion requires that the OT passage be identifiable by wording, not just topic, and that invoking it serves a clear rhetorical or doctrinal purpose in Christ's statement.
2. **D&C inclusion** — Deferred to Phase 1. Will include if the count is manageable and the instances add meaningfully to the study.
3. **3 Nephi OT source** — Christ in 3 Nephi sometimes quotes from the Brass Plates version of OT texts, which may differ from MT/LXX/KJV wording. Need to note and document these differences without over-interpreting them.
4. **Quotation classification scheme** — Need to decide on a consistent taxonomy (e.g., explicit formula, direct quote, paraphrase, verbal allusion) and apply it uniformly across all entries.
