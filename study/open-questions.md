# The Chosen Word — Open Questions

**Last updated:** 2026-10-22
**Status:** Phase 1 complete — carried forward to Phase 2 and beyond

These are unresolved questions that emerged during Phase 1 research. They do not block Gate 1 or architecture, but they affect how certain passages are described on the site. Each is flagged with what is needed to resolve it.

---

## Requiring Official Church Source Verification

**QO-1. Hos 6:6 — Framing escalation**
Does the shift from "go ye and learn what that meaneth" (Matt 9:13) to "if ye had known what this meaneth" (Matt 12:7) represent deliberate escalation by Christ, or simply two different rhetorical tones for two different occasions?
- What's needed: Conference talk, institute manual, or official commentary that addresses these two citations together or that discusses Christ's use of Hosea 6:6
- Where flagged: PASS-002-hos-6-6.md

**QO-2. Ps 110:1 — "Thou hast said" at the trial**
Is "Thou hast said" (Matt 26:64) a full affirmation of the high priest's question in LDS understanding, or a qualified or ambiguous response?
- What's needed: JST check on Matt 26:64; institute or seminary manual commentary on the trial
- Where flagged: PASS-003-ps-110-1.md

**QO-3. Dan 7:13 — Adam presiding at Christ's investiture**
Now that D&C 116, 107:53–57, and 138:38 establish Adam as the Ancient of Days, are there official Church sources that discuss specifically what it means that Adam (father of humanity) presides over the moment Christ receives universal eternal dominion? The connection is documented in PASS-004; the deeper theological significance is not yet sourced.
- What's needed: Conference talk or Teachings of the Prophet Joseph Smith that addresses the Adam-ondi-Ahman gathering in relation to Daniel 7
- Where flagged: PASS-004-dan-7-13.md

**QO-4. Lev 19:18 — "Love your enemies" as restoration or replacement**
The antithesis extension to enemies — is this Christ restoring the original intent of Lev 19:18 (the chapter already commands love of the stranger) or revealing something entirely new? PASS-001 notes this is a possible reading but defers to official sources.
- What's needed: General Conference talk or Gospel Topics article on the Sermon on the Mount / higher law
- Where flagged: PASS-001-lev-19-18.md

## Catalog Classification — Pending Chase Decisions

**QO-5. Beatitudes and Psalm 37**
Do any of the Beatitudes (Matt 5:3–12) contain OT allusions specific enough to catalog? Psalm 37 in particular contains parallel language ("the meek shall inherit the earth," Ps 37:11 / Matt 5:5). If Christ is citing Ps 37 in the Beatitudes, these would be additional entries.
- Decision needed: Include beatitude/Ps 37 connections or treat as thematic echoes?

**QO-6. 3 Ne 20:14–21 — Micah 4 echoes**
These verses contain gathering-of-sheaves and iron-horns language that echoes Micah 4:12–13. They were not cataloged because the 3 Ne text blends Christ's prophetic speech with OT echoes rather than quoting clearly. 
- Decision needed: Add as catalog entries, or leave as noted gaps?

**QO-7. D&C tentative entries — five entries flagged**
DC-003 (cup of indignation / Jer 25), DC-007 (Isa 9:2 light), DC-010 (Gen 1 waters), DC-011 (Gen 3 curse), DC-014 (four winds / Zech 2 + Isa 11) are classified tentative — the verbal parallels are real but less close than the high-confidence entries.
- Decision needed: Accept all 17 D&C entries, or review and potentially remove the five tentative ones?

**QO-8. "Great I Am" — D&C 39:1**
"The Great I Am" (D&C 39:1) echoes Exodus 3:14. If the catalog treats divine-title self-identification as an OT citation type, this may warrant inclusion. Currently excluded as a thematic echo.
- Decision needed: Does "I AM" qualify as a citation type for this catalog?

## Textual / Scholarly Questions

**QO-9. 3 Nephi Brass Plates wording differences**
Christ in 3 Nephi sometimes quotes from Brass Plates versions of OT texts, which differ from KJV wording. This was noted during Phase 0 but not systematically addressed. When the site shows 3 Nephi OT citations alongside their KJV OT sources, wording differences will be visible.
- Decision needed: Note differences matter-of-factly on the site, or address them explicitly in the passage descriptions?

**QO-10. Sermon on the Mount — "Hate thine enemy" source**
Matt 5:43 includes "and hate thine enemy" — wording not in Leviticus. Christ corrects it, but we don't have a clear source for where this half of the antithesis comes from (rabbinic tradition? common paraphrase? something else?). Currently the record notes "interpretive tradition, not a biblical verse."
- What's needed: For the site, this should be described accurately without overstating certainty about its origin.

**QO-11. John 7:38 — composite allusion source**
Christ says "as the scripture hath said, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water" — but no single OT text matches. Possible sources noted: Isa 55:1, Zech 14:8, Ezek 47:1. Currently classified as composite allusion.
- Decision needed: How to describe this on the site without claiming a specific OT source that isn't verified.

## For Phase 2 / Site Design

**QO-12. D&C section — display treatment**
D&C entries are kept separate from Gospels/3 Nephi. The site should make this distinction clear to visitors without requiring them to understand LDS canonical history. What label or framing best communicates "latter-day revelation" vs. "mortal ministry records"?

**QO-13. Tentative entries — public display**
Several entries (5 D&C tentative, a few Gospel close allusions) have lower confidence. Should the site display a confidence indicator, or simply present all cataloged entries uniformly and let the notes carry the caveat?
