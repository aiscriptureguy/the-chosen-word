## Priority 2 — Patterns and Distribution

**Q3. Which OT books does Christ cite most frequently?** `[studied - approved 2026-10-21]`
- Tally citations by book after Q1 is complete
- Note whether heavy use of certain books (Deuteronomy, Psalms, Isaiah) reflects the prominence of those books in Second Temple Judaism or something specific to Christ's teaching priorities

**Q4. Does Christ quote the same OT passage more than once?**
- Identify repeated passages across different Gospels and across Gospels vs. 3 Nephi
- For each repeated passage, compare the settings: Who is the audience? What is the occasion? Does the purpose of the quotation shift?

**Q5. Are there OT books or major themes conspicuously absent from Christ's quotations?**
- After the catalog is complete, note gaps: which books of the OT does Christ never cite?
- Reflect on whether absence is meaningful or simply a function of the record

## Priority 3 — Purpose and Meaning (per passage)

**Q6. Why did Christ consider each scripture worth quoting?**
For each entry:
- What is the immediate occasion or question?
- What does the OT passage teach in its original context?
- How does Christ use it — as proof, as reproof, as fulfillment, as principle, as self-identification?
- What does the choice reveal about Christ's understanding of his own mission?
- What spiritual principle is he pressing the listener toward?

**Q7. How does the audience and setting affect which scriptures Christ cites?** `[studied - approved 2026-10-21]`
- Group by audience type: hostile questioners, sincere seekers, private disciples, general crowd, Nephite multitude
- Does Christ draw from different OT sources depending on who he is speaking to?

---

## Priority 4 — 3 Nephi as a Special Case

**Q8. What does the 3 Nephi record add or confirm?**
- Which passages does Christ quote in 3 Nephi that he also quotes in the Gospels? Does the wording differ?
- Which passages appear only in 3 Nephi?
- Does the 3 Nephi material suggest a different selection priority than the Gospel accounts?
- Note any instances where Christ comments on what should be written vs. what has been omitted
